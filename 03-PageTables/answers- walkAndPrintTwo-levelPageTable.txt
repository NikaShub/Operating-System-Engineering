
Explanation of vmprint output and its relation to print_pgtbl:

The vmprint function outputs the entire page table tree hierarchically 
- Non-leaf PTEs point to lower-level page tables and lack R, W, and X permissions.
- Leaf PTEs point to the actual physical pages and contain permissions (like R, W, X, U). 
The leaf pages printed by vmprint correspond directly to the same user-space and system pages we 
inspected earlier in print_pgtbl The virtual addresses and page permissions match, 
but vmprint shows the full tree structure and how the
hardware navigates from the root page table down to each specific physical page.
